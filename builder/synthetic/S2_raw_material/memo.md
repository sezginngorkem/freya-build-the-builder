HARBOR CREDIT UNION — INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

Memo no.: CO-26-14
Date issued: 2026-10-19
From: Collections Policy Office (on behalf of the Board Risk Committee)
To: Member Collections, Contact Centre Operations, Digital Channels
Subject: Amendments to POL-COL-07 v3.2 (Member Collections Policy)
Effective date: 2026-11-02 (all calls placed on or after this date)

---

Following the Q3 review of roll rates and the regulator's feedback on contact intensity, the Committee has approved the amendments below. Unless stated, every other provision of POL-COL-07 v3.2 remains in force unchanged.

**Amendment 1 — Section 5.1, Stage 2 deadline.**
For Stage 2 (16 to 30 days past due) only, the deadline is reduced from the 6th to the 4th business day after the call date. Stage 1 and Stage 3 deadlines are not changed.

**Amendment 2 — Section 5.1, Stage 3 persuasion attempts.**
For Stage 3 (31 to 60 days past due) the number of persuasion attempts is reduced from 2 to 1. Attempt 2 of section 6.6 therefore applies to Stage 2 only.

**Amendment 3 — Section 5.2, holidays.**
2026-11-27 (the day after Thanksgiving) is added to the list of Harbor Credit Union holidays. Branches and the payments desk are closed that day.

**Amendment 4 — Code list, hardship reason.**
The reason code `hardship` is renamed `financial_hardship` everywhere it is used: as a closing reason with `no_promise`, as a closing reason with `transferred`, and as a note reason. The meaning is unchanged. The old code is retired and will be rejected by the systems from the effective date. The Collections Code List has been updated accordingly.

**For information only (not yet approved).**
The Committee will consider in December whether to reduce the Stage 1 deadline to the 8th business day from 2027-01-04. Until a further memo is issued, section 5.1 applies to Stage 1 as written.
