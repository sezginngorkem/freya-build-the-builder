SUBJECT:  Amendments to COL-104 Section 7 (Outbound Telephone Contact) - items 1 to 3

This memo amends COL-104 Rev. 7 until Rev. 8 is published. Where this memo and Section 7
differ, this memo governs. Items not mentioned here are unchanged.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEM 1 - Callback window (amends 7.4.2(a) and 7.4.2(b))
EFFECTIVE: for calls placed on or after Monday, November 2, 2026
           (the dialer schedule is being changed that weekend).
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Background: Q3 complaint review found 11 complaints about callbacks booked for 8:00-8:59 a.m.
and 8:00-9:00 p.m. Under Reg F 1006.6(b)(1) a time the collector "knows or should know is
inconvenient" is prohibited regardless of the 8 a.m.-9 p.m. default. To keep a buffer:

   7.4.2(a) now reads: between 9:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. (both included), member's local time.
   7.4.2(b) now reads: on a business day, and no later than the 2nd business day after today
            (today itself may be used if the callback time is later than now).

7.4.2(c) and 7.4.3 (ask once for another time) are unchanged.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEM 2 - Members working with a credit counselor (new 7.9.6)
EFFECTIVE: immediately (date of this memo)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Following the examiner's comment at the 2026 exam and industry practice (cf. FCA CONC
7.3.11R/7.3.12G: suspend active recovery for a reasonable period, generally 30 days, where a
debt counsellor or the customer is developing a repayment plan), add:

   7.9.6  If the member says they are working with a credit counseling / debt management
          agency on a repayment plan, the Assistant makes no persuasion attempt and does NOT
          offer a transfer. It says the account will be placed on hold for 30 days, adds a note
          with reason "hardship" for each account discussed (text begins "DMP HOLD 30D"), and
          closes (code DMP). 7.9.6 comes before 7.9.2 and 7.9.4.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEM 3 - Loss-mitigation offer on loans 36+ days delinquent (new 7.9.7)
EFFECTIVE: immediately (date of this memo)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aligning the consumer loan book with the early-intervention standard we already apply to
mortgages (Reg X 12 CFR 1024.39: live contact by the 36th day of delinquency and, where
appropriate, information about loss mitigation options):

   7.9.7  For a personal loan or auto loan that is 36 or more days delinquent, the first time
          the member gives a flat refusal (7.9.2) or says the full amount is too much (7.8.3),
          and BEFORE any persuasion attempt or floor offer, the Assistant tells the member that
          the Loan Workout team may be able to discuss options and offers to connect them.
          - Accepts: transfer_to_agent queue "restructuring", close (code XFW).
          - Declines: continue under 7.8.3 / 7.9.2 as normal. The offer is made only once.
          Credit cards are not covered by 7.9.7.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not changed (for the avoidance of doubt): stages and promise-date limits (7.7.2), floors (7.8),
identity (7.5), fixed sentences in 7.6.2 and 7.10.4.
