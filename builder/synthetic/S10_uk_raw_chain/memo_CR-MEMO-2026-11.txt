PENNINE & WHARFE BANK PLC                                       INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To:       Collections & Recoveries; Contact Centre Operations; Digital Voice (Automated Assistant)
From:     Head of Collections & Recoveries, with Compliance (R. Price)
Date:     05/10/2026
Subject:  Temporary changes to CRP-07 Part 5 - items 1 to 4

This memo amends CRP-07 Part 5 (Rev. 4) until Rev. 5 is published. Where this memo and Part 5
differ, this memo applies. Anything not mentioned here is unchanged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEM 1 - Band 2 promise-date limit (amends 5.7.2)
EFFECTIVE: calls placed on or after Monday 12/10/2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Band 2 right-party contact rates have dropped since the August price change. To give Band 2
customers a little more room to align a promise with pay dates, the Band 2 promise-date limit is
the 6th business day after today (instead of the 5th). Bands 1 and 3 are unchanged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEM 2 - Core banking migration (amends 5.7.1)
EFFECTIVE: calls placed on or after Monday 12/10/2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Bank will be closed on Friday 20/11/2026 for the core banking migration. Payments cannot be
taken or dated on that day. For this Part, 20/11/2026 is NOT a business day, and no callback may
be booked for it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEM 3 - Customers receiving debt advice (new 5.10.5)
EFFECTIVE: calls placed on or after Monday 12/10/2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONC 7.3.11R requires us to suspend active pursuit of a debt for a reasonable period where the
customer, or someone acting for them, is developing a repayment plan; CONC 7.3.12G says this
should generally be 30 days. Add:

   5.10.5  If the customer says they are already getting help from a debt advice provider
           (for example StepChange, Citizens Advice, National Debtline, Christians Against
           Poverty, or a named debt adviser) and the adviser is putting together or has set up
           a repayment plan (including a "debt management plan"), the Assistant:
             - makes no further payment request and no persuasion attempt, and does NOT offer a
               transfer;
             - says: "We will put your account on hold for 30 days while you get debt advice.";
             - ends every account without an agreed promise with code DAH.
           Promises already agreed for earlier accounts are still read back and recorded.
           5.10.5 comes before 5.10.1 and 5.11.3. It does not apply if the customer only says
           they are thinking of contacting a debt adviser.

Ops: code DAH has been added to Appendix C.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ITEM 4 - Saturday callbacks (amends 5.4.2(b))
EFFECTIVE: calls placed on or after Monday 12/10/2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Saturday shift is withdrawn while the migration team is staffed from Collections. No callback
may be booked for a Saturday. 5.4.2(b) now reads: "Monday to Friday between 09:00 and 20:00 (both
ends included) - never on a Saturday, a Sunday, a bank holiday (5.7.1) or a day the Bank is
closed." 5.4.2(a), 5.4.2(c) and 5.4.3 are unchanged.

Questions to collections.policy@pennine-wharfe.example. Please do not reply to all.
