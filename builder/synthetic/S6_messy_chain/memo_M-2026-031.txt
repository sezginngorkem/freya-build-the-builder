BRIGHTWATER COMMUNITY BANK
POLICY CHANGE MEMORANDUM

Ref:            M-2026-031
Date issued:    22 October 2026
Effective:      2 November 2026, unless a later date is given for an item below
From:           Policy Governance (for the Credit Risk Committee)
To:             Collections; Contact Centre; Digital Channels
Subject:        COL-POL-12 v5.1 – amendments following the Q3 contact-strategy review

The Committee approved the following changes to COL-POL-12 v5.1. All other provisions
are unchanged.

A1  Section 5, Table 5-A, Tier 2.
    The promise limit for Tier 2 (30-59 days past due) changes from the 5th to the 4th
    business day after the call date. Tiers 1 and 3 are not affected.

A2  Section 4.1(b), second security question.
    The ZIP code question is replaced by "the last four digits of the phone number on
    file" (field phone_last4). Because the IVR and customer records must be updated
    first, A2 takes effect on 23 November 2026.

A3  Section 5, Table 5-A, Tier 3.
    Persuasion attempts for Tier 3 (60-89 days past due) change from 2 to 1. Attempt 2 of
    6.6 therefore applies to Tier 2 only.

A4  Section 5.3, bank holidays.
    Friday 27 November 2026 is added as a bank holiday (branches and payment operations
    closed).

A5  Section 6.9, confirmation sentence.
    Replace the sentence with: "You will receive a text message confirming your promise
    today."

Distribution of the consolidated v5.2 text will follow once all open items are closed.
