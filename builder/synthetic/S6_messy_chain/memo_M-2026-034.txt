BRIGHTWATER COMMUNITY BANK
POLICY CHANGE MEMORANDUM

Ref:            M-2026-034
Date issued:    12 November 2026
Effective:      16 November 2026 (all calls on or after this date), except item 5
From:           Policy Governance (for the Credit Risk Committee)
To:             Collections; Contact Centre; Digital Channels
Subject:        Amendments to M-2026-031 and to COL-POL-12 v5.1
Supersedes:     M-2026-031 items A1 and A2 only

1.  M-2026-031 A1 (Tier 2 promise limit) is replaced. From the effective date of this memo
    the Tier 2 promise limit is the 3rd business day after the call date. (Between 2 and
    15 November 2026 the 4th business day under M-2026-031 A1 applied.)

2.  M-2026-031 A2 (phone_last4 security question) is withdrawn before taking effect. The
    IVR change has been postponed with no new date. Section 4.1(b) of the policy (ZIP code)
    continues to apply.

3.  For the avoidance of doubt, M-2026-031 A3, A4 and A5 remain in force.

4.  New section 7.6 – requests to stop calling.
    If the customer asks the bank to stop calling them, the agent says "Understood. We will
    stop calling you about this account.", makes no further request or offer, adds a note
    with reason cease_contact for every product without a promise, and closes the call No
    promise / cease_contact. One request is enough. This is not a hardship case and not a
    refusal under 6.6.

5.  Tier 1 promise limit – NOT YET IN FORCE.
    Subject to ALCO sign-off, from 1 December 2026 the Tier 1 promise limit will become the
    6th business day after the call date. Until then Table 5-A applies to Tier 1 as
    written. A further memo will confirm.
