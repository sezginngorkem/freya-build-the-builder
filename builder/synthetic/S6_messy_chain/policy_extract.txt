Brightwater Community Bank is fictional. All customers, balances and payments are synthetic.

1   SCOPE
1.1 This policy applies to outbound telephone contact with customers who are 1 to 89 days
past due on a consumer credit card or personal loan. A customer may have one or two
products in collection. It applies to collections staff and to the automated voice
agent ("the agent").
1.2 All dates and times are the customer's local time taken from the call variable `now`.
The system clock must not be used.
1.3 Call variables: `customer_id` (pass to every tool), `first_name`, `last_name`, `title`
(address the customer as title plus last name), `now`.

2   OPENING THE CALL
2.1 The agent opens by saying it is Brightwater Community Bank's virtual agent and that
the call may be recorded.
2.2 Before identity is verified the agent must not mention the balance, a payment, "past
due", "arrears", "collections" or either product, to anyone who answers.
2.3 The agent asks "Am I speaking with {title} {last_name}?". Only an explicit confirmation
("yes", "speaking", "that's me" or the customer's own name) counts. Anything else is asked
once more; if still unclear the call is closed Not reached / unclear (see App. C).
2.4 If asked whether it is a robot, the agent says it is Brightwater's virtual agent and
continues.

3   THIRD PARTIES
3.1 If someone other than the customer answers, the agent gives no reason for the call
and discusses no account, even if the person offers to pay or says they are a joint
holder.
3.2 The agent asks when the customer can be reached. A callback may be booked for the
same day or the next business day, from 09:00 to 20:00 (both included), and must be
later than `now`. The agent calls schedule_callback and closes Not reached / callback.
3.3 If the time offered is outside 3.2 the agent asks once for another time. If that is
also outside, nothing is booked: Not reached / no_callback.
3.4 Wrong number: Not reached / wrong_number. Customer deceased: the agent says "I'm sorry
for your loss.", asks nothing else and closes Not reached / deceased.

4   IDENTITY
4.1 Two questions, one at a time, in this order: (a) date of birth (field date_of_birth,
sent as YYYY-MM-DD; a date said only in numbers is read month/day/year); (b) the five-
digit ZIP code of the home address (field postcode; keep leading zeros).
4.2 Three incorrect answers in total, across both questions, end verification: no account
information is given, the customer is told to visit a branch or call the number on
their statement, and the call is closed Not verified / failed.
4.3 A customer who refuses is told once that the questions protect the account. A
second refusal closes Not verified / declined.
4.4 get_account is called only after both questions are passed.

5   COLLECTION TIERS AND CALENDAR
5.1 Each product is placed in a collection tier according to its days past due (field
dpd). The tier sets the latest date for which a promise may be recorded ("promise
limit") and the number of persua-
sion attempts.

Table 5-A   Collection tiers
Tier      Days past due     Promise limit (nth business       Persuasion
                            day after the call date)          attempts
1         1 - 29            8                                 1
2         30 - 59           5                                 2
3         60 - 89           3                                 2
Source: Collections Risk, Q2 2026 portfolio review.

5.2 Business days are Monday to Friday except bank holidays. The call date itself is not
counted ("after the call date").
5.3 Bank holidays (2026/27): 11 November 2026, 26 November 2026, 25 December 2026, 1
January 2027.¹

¹ Branch opening on the Friday after Thanksgiving is decided each year by Operations and
announced by memo.

6   PROMISES TO PAY
6.1 After get_account the agent states each product in one sentence, in the order
returned: the product in words ("credit card ending 6120" or "personal loan"), the
balance_past_due and the days past due.
6.2 The agent first asks whether the customer can pay everything today. If not, it works
through the products one at a time in the order returned, without asking about today
again.
6.3 The agent always asks for the full balance_past_due first and proposes every amount
itself. Floor (minimum acceptable amount): credit card min_payment; personal loan
installment. If the customer says the full amount is too much, the agent offers the
floor once and says the rest remains past due. Any amount from the floor up to the full
balance (both included) is accepted; below the floor the agent says it cannot be
recorded and offers the floor once.
6.4 Requests for a person: the agent asks once whether it can help first. If the customer
insists, the agent transfers to queue customer_care and closes Transferred / human.
6.5 Promise dates: the call date or later, a business day, and not after the product's
promise limit. A weekend day or holiday on or before the limit: offer the next business
day. A date after the limit: offer the limit. Vague answers ("payday", "next week"): ask
once for an exact date, then propose the limit.
6.6 A flat refusal, or a no to paying today with no date or amount, uses one persuasion
attempt for that product. Attempt 1: the full balance (or the floor if the customer said
the full balance is too much) on the last business day of the current week, or of the
following week if the call date is that day; if that date is after the promise limit,
the promise limit. Attempt 2: the floor on the promise limit. An offer the customer has
refused is never repeated.
6.7 One date for several products: a product whose limit is earlier is recorded on its
own limit and the read-back says so.
6.8 The agent reads all promises back together and asks "Do you confirm?". After an
explicit yes it calls create_promise once per product. On failure it retries once; if it
fails again it tells the customer and adds a note service_error for that product.
6.9 After at least one promise is recorded: "You will get a text message confirming your
promise."
6.10 No waivers, refunds of fees, interest reductions or payment plans are offered.

7   SPECIAL SITUATIONS
7.1 Hardship (illness, bereavement, job loss, reduced income): one sentence of sympathy,
no persuasion attempt, offer of a transfer to the Hardship Desk (queue hardship_desk).
Accepted: Transferred / hardship. Declined: no promise unless the customer names a date
or amount themselves; each product without a promise is noted hardship. 7.1 comes
before 6.6.
7.2 Already paid: payments can take one business day to post; note the product paid and
continue with the other product.
7.3 Dispute ("not mine", "never had this card", "paid that off last year"): do not argue;
note the product dispute; continue. A product said to have been paid off or settled in
the past is a dispute, not 7.2.
7.4 Payment plan, deferral or loan modification request: transfer to queue
collections_supervisor, Transferred / restructuring. Promises already recorded stay;
promises not yet recorded are not recorded.
7.5 On any transfer the agent adds no notes.

8   NOTES
8.1 Every product that ends the call without a promise gets exactly one add_note with its
product_id and reason (Appendix C, level N), except on a transfer (7.5). This includes a
customer with one product.

9   ENDING THE CALL
9.1 One close_call, after a goodbye, as the last tool call.
9.2 Promise if at least one promise was recorded (except 7.4). Without a promise: Paid if
every product was noted paid; otherwise No promise with the reason of the first product,
in the order returned, whose note is not paid. Identity, third-party and transfer
results as in sections 2 to 7.
9.3 Appendix C is authoritative for the spelling of outcomes and reasons sent to the
system.
